// 01 WHAT IS THIS

        
PIXELVERSE turns four core computing topics into explorable zones. Read short lessons, then prove what you've learned with quizzes, flashcards, drag-and-drop challenges, and terminal mini-games.

      

      

        
// 02 HOW PROGRESS WORKS

        
Every completed lesson earns XP. XP fills your level bar and unlocks badges. Everything is saved to this browser automatically — no account, no server, just your local save file.
